What is Novrex?

Novrex is the best place to Imagine with Friends. With a user-generated online gaming platform and worlds created by users, players come to Novrex every day to create adventures, play games, role play, and learn with their friends in an immersive, 3D environment.

Novrex on your Device

You can access Novrex on PC, Mac, iOS, Android, Amazon Devices, and Xbox One. Novrex adventures are accessible from any device, so players can imagine with their friends regardless of where they are.
